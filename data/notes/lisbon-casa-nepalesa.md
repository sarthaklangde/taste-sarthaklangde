# Casa Nepalesa

Raw notes from Sarthak, verbatim. Newest last. The JSON in data/places/ is derived from these.

## 2026-10-17

> My fav place to eat desi food in town is actually Casa Nepalesa - good for date night as well.
>
> Curry - Kukhura Ra Bholte Khursani - Can get extra spicy if you want - So delish pair with Desi Roti or Malahi Roti
>
> Grill/appetizer - Kukhurako Chhati Bhungroma - Elevated version of chicken tikka - So tasty. 
>
> They use weird names to Indian restaurants but service is so good and so hospital. Like the ambience as well.
